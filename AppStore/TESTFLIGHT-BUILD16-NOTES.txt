Fixes found in build 15. Changing your memory answer later the same day (in Explore or Phrase notes) now replaces the first answer and reschedules the phrase; before, a second Hard, Good or Easy did nothing, and the intervals under the buttons changed when you tapped. The interval shown is the one saved. Tapping a review reminder now opens the review even when a sheet, practice or another tab is open. Cancelling Restore purchases no longer shows an error, a verb group opened from Saved shows only saved phrases, streaks survive daylight-saving days, and double taps in Practice can no longer skip a step or crash.

Everything else matches build 15: ratings are recorded on the tap, English glosses, the Home phrasal-verb / idiom filter, the rating row in Phrase notes, the Stats month calendar, the Settings tab and the four-collection Phrases screen. Scene and story practice are not reachable in this build.
